## Fit warning — worth reading before you spend the time

This posting is not on your target list, and the screening questions are the reason.
"Custom engineered equipment through full lifecycle," "budget management years," and
"average budget size" are the signature of an **industrial / mechanical equipment project
manager** role — capital projects, physical hardware, vendor and cost control. Rheo
Engineering builds rheology and materials-testing equipment.

Answering honestly, you land at: **No** on equipment lifecycle, **0** on budget management,
**smallest bracket** on budget size, and the **lowest** band on PM years. Those four are
almost certainly knockout questions — forms ask them precisely to filter.

Three of those cannot be improved by framing; they're facts about experience you don't have
yet. That's not a knock on you — it's a different career track from the one your resume
supports.

**My recommendation: skip this one** and put the hour into the queue instead. Amex Data
Engineer I (Phoenix, your strongest lead), Oracle, Hipp Health, Meds, Revel. Those are roles
where honest answers make you competitive rather than screening you out.

If you want to apply anyway — your call, and it costs you one form — send the block above
as written. Do not inflate the equipment or budget answers. A false "Yes" on equipment
lifecycle surfaces in the first ten minutes of a phone screen and ends the conversation
worse than a "No" ever would.
