## Overview

The V1 gift catalogue (from the sprint brief) has ~25 items across 4 categories: Care Items, Accessories, Outfits, and Special Gifts. This document proposes **15 new gifts** for the V2 backlog, inspired by real mechanics from SHOWROOM, Pococha, TikTok LIVE, and Hololive/Nijisanji fan culture.

All gifts are rooted in Japanese oshi-katsu (fan devotion) culture and idol streaming traditions.

---

## New Category: Fan Items

This category doesn't exist in V1 but is core to idol culture. SHOWROOM and Pococha both have "cheering" gift categories that let fans express support (not just give food/accessories). These are high-frequency, low-cost items that drive daily engagement.

## Price Curve Design

The price distribution is intentional:

```
5–25 coins   (4 items)  → Daily habit gifts, high frequency, low revenue per transaction
50–150 coins  (6 items)  → "Treat" gifts, weekly frequency, meaningful Genki boost
300–1,000 coins (5 items) → Statement gifts, whale targeting, dramatic reactions
```

This matches the SHOWROOM/Pococha economic model where:
- ~80% of transactions are low-tier (drives engagement)
- ~80% of revenue comes from high-tier (drives monetization)
