## Your position, stated plainly

You are **work-authorized today** and, with the STEM extension, for roughly **36 months**
(12 months initial OPT + 24 months STEM OPT) **with no filing, no fee, and no lawyer on the
employer's side.** That is close to a full three-year hire. The employer's only obligations are
being enrolled in E-Verify and signing Form I-983 (a training plan, not a petition).

That framing matters, because most candidates in your position answer the sponsorship question
in a way that reads as "expensive problem, starting now." Yours is "no cost for three years."

**Do not misstate this.** You *will* need H-1B sponsorship eventually, so the honest answer to
"will you now or in the future require sponsorship" is **Yes**. Answering "No" is a
misrepresentation that surfaces at I-9 and gets offers rescinded. The advantage is in the
accurate framing below, not in dodging the question.
