## Sponsorship Policy — PRIMARY FILTER

This replaces comp as the main hard filter.

**Status (confirmed 2026-07-30): F-1 initial post-completion OPT.** Work-authorized now,
12-month clock, 90-day cumulative unemployment limit. Two things follow:

1. **E-Verify is a hard prerequisite, not a preference.** The 24-month STEM OPT extension
   requires the employer to be enrolled in E-Verify — a student cannot satisfy it alone, and
   late enrollment often does not cure a denial. An employer not on E-Verify caps the runway
   at 12 months regardless of how good the role is. Surface E-Verify status in every
   evaluation; when unknown, list it as a recruiter-screen question.
2. **STEM eligibility is unresolved and consequential.** CIP **52.1201** (Management
   Information Systems, General) is **not** on the DHS STEM Designated Degree Program List.
   NAU's Franke College markets the MS MIS as "STEM-designated," implying it is coded under a
   STEM CIP (e.g. 11.0401 Information Science/Studies) rather than 52.1201 — but only the CIP
   printed on the I-20 governs. Until NAU ISSS confirms it, treat the 36-month runway as
   unconfirmed and do not let the user plan around it.

Because the initial-OPT clock is short and the unemployment limit is cumulative, favor speed
of process where quality is equal: smaller companies and Ashby/Lever forms convert faster than
large-enterprise Workday pipelines.

- **Hard exclude** postings stating: US Citizen only, no sponsorship now or in the future,
  active security clearance required, Green Card required.
- **Prioritize** known H-1B sponsors, E-Verify employers, and cap-exempt organizations
  (universities, nonprofit research institutes, teaching hospitals) — cap-exempt is a
  structural advantage worth surfacing explicitly in evaluations.
- If sponsorship stance is unstated, treat as **unknown, not disqualifying** — note it in
  the report under Block G and flag it as a question for the recruiter screen.
- Arizona State University, University of Arizona, NAU, Banner Health, Mayo Clinic Arizona,
  and TGen are all local cap-exempt employers worth standing coverage.
